# My Favorite Anime: Dr. Stone

## About Dr. Stone

**Dr. Stone** is my favorite anime. It tells the story of humanity turning into stone and how *Senku Ishigami* tries to rebuild civilization using science.

## Why I Like It

- I like how humanity is rebuilt from scratch.
- It teaches interesting scientific concepts.
- I enjoy its slow-paced storytelling.
- Every invention feels like an achievement.

## My Favorite Character

**Senku Ishigami** – I like his intelligence, creativity, and determination to bring science back to humanity.

> "This is exhilarating!"
